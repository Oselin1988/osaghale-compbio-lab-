Advancing Precision Medicine Through Computational Biology

The Osaghale CompBio Lab uses AI, machine learning, and bioinformatics to combat antimicrobial resistance, understand pathogen evolution, and translate genomic discoveries into actionable health insights.

Explore Our Research → View Publications

Research Focus

Our lab uses computational and AI‑driven approaches to address pressing challenges in infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance, and precision medicine.

🧠

AI & ML for AMR

Hybrid deep learning models (CNN–BiLSTM–Attention) for rapid AMR prediction from genomic data. AUROC 0.77 across 22 antibiotics.

TensorFlow · Keras · Python

🧬

Phylogenomics & Genomics

Large‑scale comparative genomics of bacterial pathogens. Analysed >1,255 S. aureus genomes using PGLS and Pagel's λ.

R · Python · Snippy · FastTree

🔬

Structural Bioinformatics

Protein structure prediction (AlphaFold) and molecular docking (AutoDock Vina) for drug target identification and inhibitor discovery.

AlphaFold · AutoDock Vina · PLIP

🧪

Adaptive ML for Therapy

RNNs, Bayesian change‑point detection, and reinforcement learning for personalised treatment optimisation. 79.1% sensitivity.

RL · LSTM · Python

🌍

Public Health Genomics

One Health approaches to AMR surveillance, foodborne pathogen genomics, and environmental metagenomics.

ABRicate · ResFinder · VFDB

📊

Bioinformatics Pipelines

Reproducible workflows for GWAS, WGCNA, k‑mer feature engineering, and network analysis.

Python · R · GitHub
